4. DIY Indoor Air Quality Protocols

DIY Corsi-Rosenthal / Box Fan Filter

Tape a MERV-13 furnace filter to the intake side of a standard 20-inch box fan. Delivers 250+ CADR filtration at under 15% the cost of commercial units.

Winter Inversion Vent Window

Never ventilate between 6:00 AM - 10:00 AM or 7:00 PM - 11:00 PM when thermal inversions trap cold, soot-laden air near the ground.

Damp Mopping vs. Vacuuming

Vacuum exhaust without True-HEPA seals resuspends toxic ultra-fine heavy metal particles into breathing height. Always wet mop with microfiber.
